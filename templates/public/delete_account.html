Delete your AttendPro account

AttendPro is made by {{ site.company_name }}. You can ask us to delete your account, or your whole organisation, at any time, from the AttendPro app or this page.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form on this page (in the app: Change password → Delete my account).
  2. We confirm the request with you, and for staff and students, with your organisation's administrator, because your organisation controls its attendance records.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

Staff and students can also ask their administrator, who can remove their account straight away from Members.

What we delete

  • Your account: your name, sign-in ID, password, email, phone, photo and profile details.
  • Your attendance data: check-in and check-out times, location readings, scan records, check-in selfies and your reference face, leave requests and notifications.
  • Your payroll data (if used): pay rates, bank details and payslips.
  • For a whole organisation: all of the above for every member, plus departments, branches, settings, reports and backups.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Payment records for an organisation's plan (amount, date, reference): kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. They contain no card details.
  • Server backups: deleted data can remain in our encrypted nightly backups for up to 14 days, until they expire.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, only for as long as it requires.

See our privacy policy for more.
