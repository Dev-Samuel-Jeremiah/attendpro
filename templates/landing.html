FAQ

Questions people ask

Do staff need to install an app?

No. AttendPro runs in the phone's browser. Staff sign in once with their ID and stay signed in for 30 days, so clocking in is a single tap.

What do we need at the entrance?

Any screen with a browser for the QR code: a TV, a monitor or a tablet. Or switch the code's refresh off and print it. On the free plan you don't need anything at the entrance: staff use the Clock in button, which checks their GPS location.

Can someone clock in for a colleague?

It's made very hard. The QR code changes every 30 seconds and the Clock in button only works on your premises. Every attempt, including refused ones, is logged.

What if a phone has no GPS or no data?

If your plan includes it, they can scan the QR code at reception instead. Otherwise an administrator can mark the day as excused with a note. Clock-in and clock-out times themselves can only come from the person's own scan, so nobody can edit them.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade any time from Plan & billing. If a paid plan runs out, you move to the free plan: nothing is deleted, and paid features come back as soon as you renew.

Is our data safe?

Each organisation's data is kept separate, connections are encrypted, and the database is backed up every night. Location is only read at the moment someone clocks in. See our privacy policy.

How do we pay, and can we get a refund?

Paid plans are paid monthly or yearly (yearly costs less) through Paystack (card, bank transfer or USSD), and never renew automatically. If you're not happy, ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying for a new plan. More about billing
