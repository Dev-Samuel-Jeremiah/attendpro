FAQ

Questions people ask

Do staff need to install an app?

No. AttendPro runs in the phone's browser. Staff sign in once with their ID and stay signed in for 30 days, so clocking in is a single tap.

What do we need at the entrance?

Any screen with a browser for the QR code: a TV, a monitor or a tablet. On the free plan you don't need a screen at all: staff use the GPS Clock in button.

Can someone clock in for a colleague?

It's made very hard. The QR code changes every 30 seconds and the Clock in button only works on your premises. Every attempt, including refused ones, is logged.

What if a phone has no GPS or no data?

An administrator can add or correct any entry by hand from the daily register, with a note.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade any time from Plan & billing. If a paid plan runs out, you move to the free plan: nothing is deleted, and paid features come back as soon as you renew.

Is our data safe?

Each organisation's data is kept separate, connections are encrypted, and the database is backed up every night.
