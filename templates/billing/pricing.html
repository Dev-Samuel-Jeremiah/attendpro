Billing questions

Before you pay

Is Basic really free?

Yes, for up to 10 active accounts (administrators included), with no time limit and no card needed. It's also the best way to try AttendPro: start on Basic and upgrade whenever you're ready.

How do we pay?

Online through Paystack, by card, bank transfer or USSD, from Plan & billing in your account. Each payment covers one month. Paystack emails a receipt, and every payment is listed in Plan & billing.

Does it renew automatically?

No. Nothing is charged without you. We remind your administrators 7, 3 and 1 day before a paid month ends. If you don't renew, you move to the free plan: nothing is deleted, and paid features come back when you pay again.

Can we get a refund?

Yes. If you're not satisfied, ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying for a new plan{% if site.sales_email %} at {{ site.sales_email }}{% endif %}. After 7 days, the current month isn't refundable. See our terms.

Can we change plans?

Any time. Paying early for the same plan adds another month on top. Switching to a different plan starts it straight away.

What counts towards the staff limit?

Active accounts, including administrators. Deactivated people don't count, so you can stay within your limit by deactivating people who have left.

Do you offer a free trial of paid plans?

The free Basic plan is the trial: it has the Clock in button, the live dashboard and attendance history for up to 10 people. If you'd like to see Professional or Enterprise features first, talk to us.
